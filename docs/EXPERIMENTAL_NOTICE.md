# Experimental software notice and terms — DRAFT

**Draft for legal review.** The first section is shown inside the app (onboarding) and must stay true.

## What every user is told
Cipher is **experimental software**. Its cryptography and protocols have **not been independently audited**. It has been tested on emulators and in automated tests, **not on a representative set of physical phones**. Do not rely on it where your safety, freedom or life depends on it.
Cipher protects message content with end-to-end encryption. It does **not** make you anonymous. Servers and network observers can still learn that you use Cipher, when, and roughly how much you send. There is no account recovery: losing or resetting your phone loses your identity and messages.

## What must not be claimed
"Unhackable", "anonymous", "untraceable", "military-grade", "audited", "safe against intelligence agencies", "production ready". Claims about strength are blocked until the independent review (ST-005) and the physical-device matrix (ST-028) are complete.
